It is incredible how much this book has reminded me of Book of the New Sun, an incredible work in its own right, just due to the narrative style and peculiarities of the narrator. I felt very vindicated to find the author herself has confirmed it as a key influence. Larger write-up soon.

***9/12/26***
Hello! Back (from) the future, with an update. Not yet a full write-up, been busy.

I finished the series quite quickly -- funny, to me, since this is the second attempt and I hardly got through the second book the first time around. There are a number themes, ideas, and stylistic choices made through the works that inspire a lot of optimism. Yet, at the same time, pessimism. Dread. Anticipation of conflict arising from pieces developed in-world but still in progress. I don't know. 

It was *memorable.* It was sci-fi and not; utopian and not. Fast-travel and 20-hour weeks. The end of famine and disease, nominally. The purported end (through silence) of religious war. The supposed end (through will and cloth-over-eyes and the end of nation-states) of identitarian violence. Peace -- but though surveillence. 

At the moment, I am trying to finish up a post (or series) on de-extinction + the Colossol Direwolves -- future me should return and finish my thoughts, here, soon.
